Questions

How is my value calculated?

We start from the vehicle's typical price new, apply depreciation for its age, then adjust for mileage against the 12,000-miles-a-year average, condition, accident history, title status and options.

Is the online value final?

It's an estimate. Your final offer is confirmed after a short in-person inspection.

What if I still have a loan?

No problem. We pay off your lender directly and pay you any difference.

What do I need to sell?

Your title (or loan info), a valid ID, keys and current registration.
